About StrangerMeet

StrangerMeet connects you with real strangers instantly via peer-to-peer WebRTC video — your video never passes through our servers. We built this to recreate the magic of Omegle and OmeTV, free forever.

🔒 Privacy first: We never record video or audio. All video is direct device-to-device. Only your chat messages are briefly relayed (not stored) through our signaling server.

🛡️ Safety first: 18+ only. Zero tolerance for inappropriate content. One-tap reporting reviewed immediately. Users violating terms are permanently banned by IP.

⚙️ Tech: WebRTC (P2P video) · Socket.io (signaling) · Google STUN servers · End-to-end encrypted streams · No recordings ever

📋 Terms & Conditions

1. Age Requirement

You must be 18+ to use StrangerMeet. By registering you confirm this. Accounts found to belong to minors are immediately terminated and reported.

2. Prohibited Content

Strictly banned: nudity / sexual content, harassment, threats, hate speech, illegal activity, sharing others' personal info, impersonation. Violations = permanent ban.

3. Privacy & Data

Video/audio is 100% peer-to-peer — we never see or record it. We store only your account details (username, email, hashed password). We never sell data.

4. Safety & Reporting

Use the 🚩 Report button for inappropriate behaviour. All reports reviewed within minutes. False reports violate these terms.

5. AI Moderation

Our systems may auto-end sessions showing prohibited content. Appeals via support.

6. Liability

StrangerMeet provides infrastructure only. We're not responsible for user-generated content but act fast on reports.

7. Termination

We may ban any account at any time for violations without notice.

Contact: safety@strangermeet.app
